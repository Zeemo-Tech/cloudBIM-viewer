# BIM-guided aligned classification

The production `/alignments/bim/denoise` path accepts the existing saved scan-to-BIM matrix. It does not re-register the assets. A new result lives in its own version directory; `cleaned.las` remains an exact subset of the original scan records containing only structural steel (class 3).

## Coordinates and semantics

`bim_scene_geometry.py` loads actual IFC world triangles in metres. The full IFC → GLB mapping is `(X,Y,Z) → (X,Z,-Y)`, with determinant +1. The complete saved column-major scan-to-GLB matrix is inverted to obtain scan coordinates. GUID-matched GLB world bounds and bidirectional vertex samples validate origin, units and rotation; failed validation aborts. Individual geometry failures are reported separately from insufficient scan evidence. Duplicate design occurrences retain their GUIDs.

Scene classification requires explicit `IfcReinforcingBar` semantics. Legacy exports using plates for structural rods continue through their former path. In the repaired model, positively typed fixture, embedded and pipe objects are removed from the structural inventory, correcting non-rebar objects previously reported as unparsed bars.

## Evidence sequence

1. Fit the fixture assembly and individual component rigid poses against observed point surfaces. Maximum local shift is 80 mm and rotation 18 degrees. A regularized point-to-plane fit leaves unsupported tangential directions near their design pose. Held-out **template surface samples** check support against the same scan; this is not an independent second scan.
2. Assign only source rows within 3 mm of accepted finite triangle surfaces, with compatible measured normals. Tolerance accommodates small dimension differences; no global scale or mesh deformation is fitted. Plausible structural tube contacts stay unclaimed to protect adjacent steel. Embedded accessories and pipes receive separate classes.
3. Fit provisional structural bodies on eligible original rows, preserving the original post-table candidate scope. The explicit exclusion mask removes only supported non-rebar surface rows; it does not fabricate a semantic layer partition. The guided pipeline calls `fit_control_net(..., complete_members=False)`: curves and cut faces cannot publish ownership while their supporting bodies can still change.
4. Recover short near-vertical connectors from connected scan components. Jointly fit tilt, center and apparent radius. Alternate original rows are held out for validation. Reject radius-bound solutions, planar strips, large fixture cylinders, disconnected endpoint patches and competing design identities. Current bounds are a 60 mm center shift, 25 degree tilt and apparent/design radius ratio 0.65–1.65. The apparent radius is reported as a deviation; nominal BIM dimensions remain unchanged.
5. Cluster local cross-section modes and track them along the member axis. Each circle candidate still passes held-out surface, radius and normal checks. Match candidates one-to-one to predicted tracks using a robust local tangent from up to seven recent sections; unmatched modes start separate tracks. Keep local links within 90 mm and a transverse prediction gate `min(30 mm, max(3 mm, 1.5 × nominal radius) + 2/m × gap²)`. Multiple modes at one station do not remove its valid body points. A substantial track anchors identity; rank capped support per axial station with a bounded design-distance term, not raw point density. Grow its two ends across gaps up to `max(90 mm, min(300 mm, 25% of body length))` only when forward and backward tangent predictions agree. Fill missed interior stations from measured clusters consistent with the anchored axis. Select at most one mode per station and record candidate/selected track counts. An unselected parallel track stays available to other instances or pending; it is not globally labeled noise.
6. Check both missing and partially observed long bars in the original 150 mm corridor. Include a member's existing source owners as evidence while preserving other members' owners. Broad actual fixture panels supply full tilted interior plane equations in a frame derived from design directions. Apply lateral body limits while allowing axial protrusions within design ends plus 40 mm. A candidate must beat every competing parallel design identity by at least 3 mm, including neighbours with no scan match. This neighbourhood clips the search well before 150 mm wherever spacing is narrow. Nearly tied identities remain unassigned.
7. Cross-section circles initialize **IFC sweep-axis flexible registration**. A member needs at least 20 sections, substantial visible span and 55% station coverage in an identifying track. Pre-fit trace fragments need at least five sections and 47 mm span, plus the bidirectional continuity checks above. The solver compares the existing 6/10/14-coefficient models with a local cubic B-spline (60 mm knot spacing), optimizing fixed-radius source-surface residuals and curvature regularization. Alternate 5 mm axial spatial bands validate the solve: at least 80% surface support and median residual below 0.9 mm globally, plus at least five held-out points and 65% support in each accepted local window. Candidate discovery still uses the same scan; this is not independent field validation. Prefer additional model-consistent sections without reducing global held-out support by more than one percentage point. Model-consistent centers seed observed support. Grow to adjacent 12 mm stations on the fitted fixed-radius axis when original rows independently satisfy local distance/normal agreement, radial and normal spread, at least 12 supported points, 62% local support, and at least five held-out rows with 65% support. Recheck fixture/parallel-neighbour identity at each grown station. After adjacent growth, v6 connects independently validated fragments across bounded gaps and may recover bilateral partial-view patches under the separate gates below. A missed circle fit alone does not erase supported steel; a planar strip cannot supply new support, and an unmeasured gap never becomes an observed interval merely because its endpoints connect. Save `axialGrowth` evidence and validate final point ownership against this measured support. Unsupported intervals never acquire points. Save reconstructible parameters and separate fitted body arc length, observed supported arc length and axial interval length; the fitted body arc is bounded to 1.08 times its design axial length. This is a bounded body-length prior, not a joint exact total-material-length solve.
8. Commit an accepted flexible body model together with its complete validated source set. Replace the old body's set instead of taking its union with the new set, including when there are no newly discovered rows. Unsupported previous rows become pending, while other instances remain unchanged. A failed revision retains the old model and support atomically. Fit terminal/join pieces once for all parents after the body phase, then fit cut faces. Each dependent geometry is anchored to the final body and must validate its own measured surface; the previous blanket exclusion of multi-mode axial cells has been removed. Full curves, locally observed hook intervals and purely inferred geometry retain their separate evidence contracts.
9. Recover compatible alternative finite fixture/embedded/pipe faces after structural bodies and hooks have claimed their supported points. A finite-triangle BVH checks all distance/normal-compatible candidates when the original closest face lies within 3 mm but has an incompatible normal. Existing scene ownership, structural owners, table exclusions and design-tube contact guards take precedence. No infinite plane extension or widened distance tolerance is used.
10. Fit two local axial-end DOFs of already-supported elongated embedded parts after steel ownership is locked. Preserve the original design caps, nominal radius and fitted rigid pose. Traverse connected outer-shell source rows (6 mm links) through shape-supported 10 mm stations, bounded to 200 mm beyond each design end. The minimum-change end fit covers only validated contiguous support; an unobserved end retains its design prior. Stop at missing support, radius changes, inconsistent normals, or ownership conflicts. Thin rings, inner-hole steel, disconnected shells and transverse planes cannot authorize extension. Expose design caps, fitted model caps and observed bounds separately in `embeddedAxialExtension.flexibleRegistration`. Observed sidewall extent is not proof of a physical end face.

The central framework is flexible registration of IFC component degrees of freedom to measured point surfaces: fixture rigid pose, steel sweep-axis deformation, and embedded local axial extent. A new result stores these parameter changes by original GUID in scan coordinates. The original IFC file and saved global alignment remain immutable. Current fixture size differences are tolerated within the surface threshold; an arbitrary mesh deformation or global scale change is not implemented.

## Output contract

Classes: 0 unknown, 1 table, 2 fixture, 3 structural steel, 4 noise, 5 embedded accessory, 6 pipe. Historical five-class manifests remain readable. `scene-classification.json` contains per-GUID geometry/pose/support diagnostics and is hash-validated by the backend. `source-labels.npz` preserves one label/owner per input LAS record, enabling exact local audit. It is not the steel-only downstream LAS.

`instanceCount` is the number of design matching segments. `matchedInstanceCount` counts segments with source points. `designPhysicalBarCount` and `matchedPhysicalBarCount` count unique structural GUIDs; any nonzero observed support is a partial match, not complete recovery. The UI displays both count families.

An unresolved model parse, insufficient source rows within the bounded search, and algorithmically unresolved geometry/occlusion are different outcomes. None establishes physical absence. Unknown points stay available; they are not noise solely because they failed BIM matching.

## Validation

Use `.cloudbim/mesh-venv/bin/python` for host tests. `test_bim_guided_classification.py` covers a shifted/rotated plate beside a real tube, partial-cylinder shape controls, large-cylinder/planar negatives, disconnected evidence and exclusion ownership. Existing denoise/control/curve/endpoint tests remain applicable. Start/rebuild integrated services only through `scripts/cloudbim-dev.sh start`.

The Lumos 13/14 real-data assessment, versioned results, preservation hashes, independent local audits and true source-point figures are under `.cloudbim/assessment-20261022-classification/`. This validates that dataset and the inspected local cases, not an independent field accuracy rate or complete geometric reconstruction.

The follow-up for embedded endpoint truncation and bent long bars is separately recorded in `.cloudbim/assessment-20261022-followup/`. `test_bim_embedded_extent.py` checks local-axis endpoint continuation, inner-hole/steel preservation, disconnected surfaces, planar crossings, thin parts and finite traversal. `test_bim_bent_bars.py` checks curved source support, rigid-frame invariance, existing ownership, occluded ends, larger-cylinder/planar negatives, neighbour identity and the axial-protrusion exception. Fixture interior planes are a search prior, not a watertight collision model: actual panels have openings and the near panel has weak scan support.

The completion and competing-face revision is recorded in `.cloudbim/assessment-20261022-optimization/`. It includes regressions that fail on the previous implementation for a one-point partial owner and a 130 mm occlusion, and finite-face competition tests covering rotation, bounds and deferred structural exclusions. Historical business result directories remain immutable.

The `bim-flexible-axis-v4` ownership revision and its historical results are preserved in `.cloudbim/assessment-20261029-instance-ownership/`. Its rule rejecting entire multi-mode axial cells was found to erase valid continuous steel and is **superseded by v5 axial tracking**, recorded in `.cloudbim/assessment-20261029-axial-growth/`. `test_bim_body_ownership.py` now requires retention of the continuous body under rotated local competition, denser parallel tracks, design-nearer local clutter on a bowed body, and local circle-detection failure. Growth must stop at pure planar strips and respect parallel-neighbour identity. It also checks model/source-set replacement, zero-addition updates, failed revision preservation, serial/prefetched release equivalence and deferred member completion. The fixed-radius surface tolerances and source-row coordinate contract are unchanged. The real-data report distinguishes measured coverage from synthetic ground-truth recall; neither a point-count increase nor model consistency proves field accuracy.

`bim-flexible-axis-v6` adds bidirectional fragment connections after the v5 axis fit. Independently validated source sections can connect across at most 90 mm between station centres, using the existing 12 mm axial grid. Every skipped station must satisfy the same fixture/parallel-neighbour identity rule. An independent fragment needs at least 6 mm of measured axial span; a thin isolated ring cannot initiate a connection. Linked fragments may continue to their validated neighbours, but a gap itself never becomes observed support.

Partial-view or cluttered patches have a separate, strictly bilateral route: reliable sections must bracket the patch within the same 90 mm limit. At least 12 surface points, 6 mm axial span, 0.10 radial spread and 0.01 normal spread are required. Local and held-out surface support must each reach 0.35; normal variation must correlate with radial variation by at least 0.65 **independently in both alternating 5 mm spatial bands**, with at least five supported points in each. This route cannot extend a weak patch beyond its outer reliable anchor. Constant normals and uncorrelated noisy plane normals are rejected. The regular section route retains its 0.62 local / 0.65 held-out support and 0.055 radial/normal spread gates. Both routes retain the fixed axis, radius, per-point residual and normal-agreement gates; neither refits a model to the newly acquired points.

`flexibleRegistration.axialGrowth` records linked/bridged section counts, bounded connections and each new section's validation mode. `observedSupportIntervalsM` contains only accepted source windows, not the spans of the connecting edges. All distances and station parameters remain in metres in the existing scan-frame axis basis; there is no new coordinate transform. `test_bim_axial_fragments.py` covers two-way chains, missing gaps, identity barriers, remote fragments, thin rings, one-sided weak evidence, noisy planes, source-order/normal-sign invariance and the rotated full recovery path with exclusions and existing ownership.

`bim-flexible-axis-v8` preserves that 12 mm pass and adds an interleaved 6 mm pass only where a window contains still-unclaimed fixed-axis surface points. All bodies complete their v6 fitting and coarse ownership commits before this extra pass starts. Additional fragments therefore cannot change a later body's seed rows or fitted axis. The late pass rechecks candidate ownership, retains the unchanged model and coarse support, and acquires only still-unowned source rows; it cannot steal another body's points. Curves and end faces still run once after all body support has settled.

This resolves short valid fragments split across the old window boundaries without changing the radius, residual, normal, spread, support-fraction or spatial hold-out gates. Strong/weak evidence keeps the v6 distinction: a weak patch cannot become an independent refinement anchor. New connections check identity at every 6 mm station. Newly accepted windows are clipped to the measured axial extent of their supported source rows, then sorted and merged with the retained coarse intervals; extra station samples alone cannot expand observed length into an empty gap. Observed display stations for these windows are clamped to those source bounds as well.

The growth report now includes `coarseStepM`, `refinementStepM`, `adaptiveWindowsEvaluated`, `adaptiveGrownSections`, per-window `sourceRangeM`, `commitPhase` and `addedSourcePoints`. Section counts can span both grids and should not be interpreted as a physical length; use the saved intervals and supported arc length. Regression cases cover 7 mm fragments across coarse-grid boundaries, observed extent clipping, identity barriers between coarse stations, skipping refinement windows for already supported source data, and unchanged later-body fit inputs/coarse owners. Measured v6/v8 results are in `docs/research/pointcloud-adaptive-fragments-2026-10-29.md`.

## Execution performance

The `execution-v4` denoise implementation retains the geometric gates and source ownership sequence above. Scene fitting prepares one source-aligned eligible point/normal pair for all component poses. Independent component poses use a bounded thread pool; native closest-face queries use the same worker budget. Bent-bar votes suppress visited neighbourhoods in the existing spatial tree without changing greedy order or rejected-prior suppression. Sorted station windows retain the exact axial gate and restore source order before alternating training/held-out selection.

Stations and the four independent axis candidates run in separate processes, using `REBAR_SPATIAL_WORKERS` (default 8, bounded by available workers and capped at 8 for these solvers). The guided pipeline owns one process pool reused by the control net, flexible axes and terminal refit; injected pools remain caller-owned. Body and curve worker budgets must agree when a pool is injected. Numerical thread pools inside each worker are limited to one thread.

While one bar's axis candidates run, `execution-v4` prepares the next bar's section fits. Only one future bar is prefetched; the prefetch queue contains at most `8 * workers` blocks of four stations, independent of scan length. The candidate superset includes current-body owners that its revision may release. Before using prefetched results, the parent compares their source rows against the current eligible-owner mask. Every section touched by newly claimed or still-retained rows is recomputed, retaining source order for training/held-out splitting; not-yet-submitted sections use current eligibility directly. Additional repair payloads are bounded by the same prefetch horizon. Results and final bar ownership are applied in the original order, and workers never write global ownership. The fixed circle residual has an analytic Jacobian. The fixed-data 8/16-process comparison and source-row checks are recorded in `docs/research/pointcloud-denoise-parallel-2026-10-28.md`.

The v6 fragment pass reuses the fitted axis projections and candidate corridor, filters to the narrow tube neighbourhood before sorting, and obtains all section windows in one binary-search batch. Identity checks are memoized per station. It adds no spatial tree, global point-cloud copy, worker pool or per-fragment nonlinear fit; the existing eight-process fitting/prefetch schedule and ordered ownership commits remain in force. Its live-data timing and preservation evidence are recorded in `docs/research/pointcloud-fragment-link-2026-10-29.md`.

The v8 late refinement retains only narrow-corridor row IDs and small fitted models while the remaining bodies finish. It projects those rows against the fixed axis after checking current ownership, without copying the whole scan or building another tree. Existing accepted coarse windows reuse their evidence; unaccepted windows recheck the current source rows. A prefix sum of uncovered surface rows selects which interleaved windows need validation; their count is bounded by the original coarse station count. A fully supported member incurs no extra interleaved window fits. Adjacent, already accepted coarse windows overlap and reuse their established connection, avoiding a fresh identity check at every supported body midpoint. Every newly validated window and every station across a new gap still requires its own identity check. A regression bounds these calls for a long supported body with one short fragment. The refinement keeps the same request-owned worker pool and deterministic body commit order.

`native/geometry.cpp` accelerates finite-triangle alternatives and polyline projections. The Docker multi-stage build compiles it without relaxed floating-point flags. For host tests run `sh native/build.sh` from `services/mesh-service`; an absent library uses the NumPy reference path. `CLOUDBIM_GEOMETRY_REFERENCE=1` forces that fallback for comparison. Native wrappers validate array dimensions before entering C++, and queries preserve stable triangle/segment tie order. A conservative float32-aware AABB broad phase only skips points that cannot reach a passing Open3D surface.

IFC triangles use one geometry iterator with shared parsing state. Semantic design extraction omits positively typed fixture plates/members before attempting steel-cylinder extraction when reinforcing entities exist; legacy plate-only steel and unresolved proxies retain their prior path. Design cache keys distinguish that extraction mode. The optional private `CLOUDBIM_MODEL_CACHE_DIR` defaults to a per-user temporary directory. It stores content-addressed design inventories and validated IFC/GLB geometry only. Scene entries bind both file hashes, the reader version and the extraction contract; each request applies its own full saved inverse transform. New or changed inputs rebuild the entry, corrupt scene entries are rebuilt, and failed coordinate validations are never cached. Scan ownership, local fitted poses and classification results are recalculated on every request.

New manifests and backend responses include optional `stageTimingsSeconds` and `modelCacheHits`. Non-overlapping stage entries cover source hashing, design preparation, point loading, scene geometry, preprocessing, scene fitting, the control net, connectors, bent bars, curve refitting, alternative faces, embedded extent and artifact export. Their sum excludes small orchestration overhead; `elapsedSeconds` remains the complete builder measurement. Neither includes HTTP queueing or browser display. Historical results without these fields remain readable. Tests cover native/reference finite geometry, buffer reuse, dense vote work, sorted station source order, cache invalidation/full transforms and serial/parallel ownership equivalence.
