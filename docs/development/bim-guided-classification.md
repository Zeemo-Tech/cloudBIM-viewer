# BIM-guided aligned classification

The production `/alignments/bim/denoise` path accepts the existing saved scan-to-BIM matrix. It does not re-register the assets. A new result lives in its own version directory; `cleaned.las` remains an exact subset of the original scan records containing only structural steel (class 3).

## Coordinates and semantics

`bim_scene_geometry.py` loads actual IFC world triangles in metres. The full IFC → GLB mapping is `(X,Y,Z) → (X,Z,-Y)`, with determinant +1. The complete saved column-major scan-to-GLB matrix is inverted to obtain scan coordinates. GUID-matched GLB world bounds and bidirectional vertex samples validate origin, units and rotation; failed validation aborts. Individual geometry failures are reported separately from insufficient scan evidence. Duplicate design occurrences retain their GUIDs.

Scene classification requires explicit `IfcReinforcingBar` semantics. Legacy exports using plates for structural rods continue through their former path. In the repaired model, positively typed fixture, embedded and pipe objects are removed from the structural inventory, correcting non-rebar objects previously reported as unparsed bars.

## Evidence sequence

1. Fit the fixture assembly and individual component rigid poses against observed point surfaces. Maximum local shift is 80 mm and rotation 18 degrees. A regularized point-to-plane fit leaves unsupported tangential directions near their design pose. Held-out **template surface samples** check support against the same scan; this is not an independent second scan.
2. Assign only source rows within 3 mm of accepted finite triangle surfaces, with compatible measured normals. Tolerance accommodates small dimension differences; no global scale or mesh deformation is fitted. Plausible structural tube contacts stay unclaimed to protect adjacent steel. Embedded accessories and pipes receive separate classes.
3. Fit structural control lines on eligible original rows, preserving the original post-table candidate scope. The explicit exclusion mask removes only supported non-rebar surface rows; it does not fabricate a semantic layer partition.
4. Recover short near-vertical connectors from connected scan components. Jointly fit tilt, center and apparent radius. Alternate original rows are held out for validation. Reject radius-bound solutions, planar strips, large fixture cylinders, disconnected endpoint patches and competing design identities. Current bounds are a 60 mm center shift, 25 degree tilt and apparent/design radius ratio 0.65–1.65. The apparent radius is reported as a deviation; nominal BIM dimensions remain unchanged.
5. Attach only existing unclaimed points supported near the transformed curved parent path, with normal and station-continuity checks. These are local hook candidates, not evidence of a complete hook or complete bar.
6. Check both missing and partially observed long bars in the original 150 mm corridor. Include a member's existing source owners as evidence while preserving other members' owners. Broad actual fixture panels supply full tilted interior plane equations in a frame derived from design directions. Apply lateral body limits while allowing axial protrusions within design ends plus 40 mm. A candidate must beat every competing parallel design identity by at least 3 mm, including neighbours with no scan match. This neighbourhood clips the search well before 150 mm wherever spacing is narrow. Nearly tied identities remain unassigned.
7. Cross-section circles initialize **IFC sweep-axis flexible registration**. Keep multiple substantial visible chains across occlusion, retaining the 90 mm link bound within each chain. A member needs at least 20 sections, substantial visible span and 55% station coverage in an identifying chain. Once that exists, nearby short chains of at least five sections and 47 mm span can support continuation within 30 mm of its predicted axis. The solver compares the existing 6/10/14-coefficient models with a local cubic B-spline (60 mm knot spacing), optimizing fixed-radius source-surface residuals and curvature regularization. Alternate 5 mm axial spatial bands validate the solve: at least 80% surface support and median residual below 0.9 mm globally, plus at least five held-out points and 65% support in each accepted local window. Candidate discovery still uses the same scan; this is not independent field validation. Prefer additional model-consistent sections without reducing global held-out support by more than one percentage point. Final ownership requires model-consistent centers, neighbour identity, tube distance, normals and observed station intervals. Unsupported intervals never acquire points. Save reconstructible parameters and separate fitted body arc length, observed supported arc length and axial interval length; the fitted body arc is bounded to 1.08 times its design axial length. This is a bounded body-length prior, not a joint exact total-material-length solve.
8. Refit terminal/join pieces for parents whose body pose changed, using the established analytic IFC curve solver and the updated attachment frames. Preserve already assigned source owners. Replace stale curve reports with the new supported or explicitly inferred geometry.
9. Recover compatible alternative finite fixture/embedded/pipe faces after structural bodies and hooks have claimed their supported points. A finite-triangle BVH checks all distance/normal-compatible candidates when the original closest face lies within 3 mm but has an incompatible normal. Existing scene ownership, structural owners, table exclusions and design-tube contact guards take precedence. No infinite plane extension or widened distance tolerance is used.
10. Fit two local axial-end DOFs of already-supported elongated embedded parts after steel ownership is locked. Preserve the original design caps, nominal radius and fitted rigid pose. Traverse connected outer-shell source rows (6 mm links) through shape-supported 10 mm stations, bounded to 200 mm beyond each design end. The minimum-change end fit covers only validated contiguous support; an unobserved end retains its design prior. Stop at missing support, radius changes, inconsistent normals, or ownership conflicts. Thin rings, inner-hole steel, disconnected shells and transverse planes cannot authorize extension. Expose design caps, fitted model caps and observed bounds separately in `embeddedAxialExtension.flexibleRegistration`. Observed sidewall extent is not proof of a physical end face.

The central framework is flexible registration of IFC component degrees of freedom to measured point surfaces: fixture rigid pose, steel sweep-axis deformation, and embedded local axial extent. A new result stores these parameter changes by original GUID in scan coordinates. The original IFC file and saved global alignment remain immutable. Current fixture size differences are tolerated within the surface threshold; an arbitrary mesh deformation or global scale change is not implemented.

## Output contract

Classes: 0 unknown, 1 table, 2 fixture, 3 structural steel, 4 noise, 5 embedded accessory, 6 pipe. Historical five-class manifests remain readable. `scene-classification.json` contains per-GUID geometry/pose/support diagnostics and is hash-validated by the backend. `source-labels.npz` preserves one label/owner per input LAS record, enabling exact local audit. It is not the steel-only downstream LAS.

`instanceCount` is the number of design matching segments. `matchedInstanceCount` counts segments with source points. `designPhysicalBarCount` and `matchedPhysicalBarCount` count unique structural GUIDs; any nonzero observed support is a partial match, not complete recovery. The UI displays both count families.

An unresolved model parse, insufficient source rows within the bounded search, and algorithmically unresolved geometry/occlusion are different outcomes. None establishes physical absence. Unknown points stay available; they are not noise solely because they failed BIM matching.

## Validation

Use `.cloudbim/mesh-venv/bin/python` for host tests. `test_bim_guided_classification.py` covers a shifted/rotated plate beside a real tube, partial-cylinder shape controls, large-cylinder/planar negatives, disconnected evidence and exclusion ownership. Existing denoise/control/curve/endpoint tests remain applicable. Start/rebuild integrated services only through `scripts/cloudbim-dev.sh start`.

The Lumos 13/14 real-data assessment, versioned results, preservation hashes, independent local audits and true source-point figures are under `.cloudbim/assessment-20261022-classification/`. This validates that dataset and the inspected local cases, not an independent field accuracy rate or complete geometric reconstruction.

The follow-up for embedded endpoint truncation and bent long bars is separately recorded in `.cloudbim/assessment-20261022-followup/`. `test_bim_embedded_extent.py` checks local-axis endpoint continuation, inner-hole/steel preservation, disconnected surfaces, planar crossings, thin parts and finite traversal. `test_bim_bent_bars.py` checks curved source support, rigid-frame invariance, existing ownership, occluded ends, larger-cylinder/planar negatives, neighbour identity and the axial-protrusion exception. Fixture interior planes are a search prior, not a watertight collision model: actual panels have openings and the near panel has weak scan support.

The completion and competing-face revision is recorded in `.cloudbim/assessment-20261022-optimization/`. It includes regressions that fail on the previous implementation for a one-point partial owner and a 130 mm occlusion, and finite-face competition tests covering rotation, bounds and deferred structural exclusions. Historical business result directories remain immutable.
